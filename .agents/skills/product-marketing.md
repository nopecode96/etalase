# Product Marketing: etalase.online

> **Single Source of Truth** for all AI Agents, Copywriters, and Growth Workflows.
> Must be read before executing any marketing, copywriting, or CRO tasks.

## 1. Product Positioning
* **Brand:** `etalase.online`
* **Category:** All-in-One Bio Link, Visual Storefront & Chat-First Commerce Platform (Southeast Asia Ready).
* **Target Audience:**
  1. **Creators & Affiliators:** Sell digital downloads, webinars, courses, and drive traffic to TikTok Shop (Keranjang Kuning), Shopee (App Oren), & Lazada.
  2. **Sales & Local SMBs:** Car dealers, real estate agents, rental, F&B cafes, and retail shops needing high-converting visual menus/showrooms with direct chat closing & 0% transaction commission.

## 2. Core Value Propositions
* **1-Click Google Onboarding:** Zero password friction, verified emails, bot-free.
* **3 Checkout Modes in 1 Store:** Instant Direct Checkout (QRIS/PayPal), External Marketplace Presets with localized SEA labels, and Direct Chat Order (WhatsApp/LINE/Zalo/Messenger).
* **WA-First Delivery & Notifications:** Instant receipt & digital download link sent via WhatsApp (powered by n8n & Fonnte).
* **Multi-Template Engine:** 8 real distinct archetypes with zero text-contrast issues.
* **Southeast Asia Localized:** Multi-language (ID, EN, MS, TH, VI), multi-currency, and localized marketplace terms.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
* **Indonesian:** Lugas, solutif, modern, berorientasi cuan tanpa jargon teknis berlebihan.
* **English:** Crisp, conversion-focused, empowering entrepreneurs & creators.
* **Malay:** Santai, profesional, mesra peniaga.
