# Skill: Conversion Copywriting

> **Pre-requisite:** Read `.agents/skills/product-marketing.md` first.

## Copywriting Framework
* **Clarity > Cleverness:** Highlight exact value (e.g. "Jualan di TikTok & WA Tanpa Potongan Komisi" vs "Ekspresikan karyamu").
* **Social Proof & Specific Numbers:** "Bergabung dengan 1.000+ Kreator & Sales di Asia Tenggara".
* **Clear CTA:** "Buat Etalase Gratis — 1 Klik via Google".
